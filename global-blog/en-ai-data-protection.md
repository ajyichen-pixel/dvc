# How to Stop Confidential Files from Reaching Generative AI Tools

Generative AI has created a new leakage path for enterprises. Employees can upload sensitive files through browsers, paste confidential text into assistants, or use personal AI accounts while trying to work faster.

## Why blocking one AI website is not enough

AI capabilities are increasingly embedded inside browsers, office suites, SaaS tools, search products, and collaboration platforms. Static domain blocklists cannot keep up with every new service or embedded feature.

A stronger model protects three things at once:

- the document itself,
- the channel through which data leaves,
- and the action taken by the user.

## Use layered controls

Persistent document protection keeps security attached to sensitive files after they are copied, renamed, emailed, or moved. DLP can monitor or restrict browser uploads, email, USB, applications, and cloud channels. De-identification can remove sensitive identifiers before legitimate AI use. Audit visibility records who attempted what, through which channel, and with which document.

## Start with high-value information

Prioritize engineering drawings, contracts, source code, customer records, financial data, strategy documents, and other files whose exposure would create immediate business impact.
