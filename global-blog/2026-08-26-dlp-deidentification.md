# DLP and Automated De-identification: Why Enterprises Need Both for Safer Generative AI

**DVC Global Security Insights · 26 August 2026**

Generative AI creates two different security decisions for enterprises. The first is whether a document or data set should be allowed to leave the organisation at all. The second is whether an approved business use can proceed after identifying information has been removed. **DLP and automated de-identification solve different parts of this problem.**

## What DLP does

DLP controls channels such as email, browsers, cloud storage, USB devices and collaboration tools. It can detect sensitive content and apply a policy to **warn, block or record** a transfer before confidential information leaves a controlled environment.

## What automated de-identification does

Automated de-identification works on the content itself. Names, phone numbers, email addresses, customer IDs, contract numbers and other identifiers can be masked or replaced while useful business context remains available for legitimate analysis.

A confidential CAD drawing headed to a public AI service should generally be stopped by DLP. An approved AI service summarising customer feedback is different: removing personal identifiers before processing can reduce exposure without preventing productive work.

## Recommended control flow

1. Classify the sensitivity of the file or data.
2. Decide whether policy should block, warn or allow the transfer.
3. De-identify approved AI inputs before processing.
4. Keep original files clearly separate from processed copies.
5. Record who handled the data, when, through which channel and to which destination.

A mature enterprise design combines **persistent document protection + DLP + automated de-identification + audit visibility**. The goal is not to choose between blocking all AI and allowing everything; it is to make the right decision for each data class and use case.
